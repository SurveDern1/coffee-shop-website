# Coffee 26

## Platform and stack
Responsive public website, Russian. Build assumption: plain HTML/CSS/ES modules, no build or runtime dependencies. Static hosting including GitHub Pages. No checkout or backend requested.

## Users and purpose
Cafe guests on phones and desktop who want to quickly find breakfast, sandwiches, coffee or another drink, compare ingredients, sizes and prices without scrolling through the entire menu.

## Brand commitments
Coffee 26. Warm, premium, quiet, straightforward copy without AI marketing clichés. User supplied three reference images and a rotating-food video. The user rejected the text-first first version. Redesign: image-led hero and category tiles; a separate full menu-category screen with a real draggable circular wheel for EVERY category, not an image swapping animation. Reference: Bean & Bloom banner and product-photo grid, plus circular-food video.

## Product truth
Menu source: supplied `coffee 26 menu.pdf`. Breakfast served until 20:00; this is not the cafe's opening hours. Ten navigation groups, with tea separated from hot drinks as requested. Currency is displayed as `р.` exactly as supplied, not inferred as BYN. Prices 1,2 and 3,5 for alternative milks are source-ambiguous: preserve the pair and ask staff for the supplement instead of assigning one to each milk. No address, contact details, opening hours or specific bean origins were provided. No invented claims, popularity tags, address, ordering or stock availability.

## Assets
User supplied layout references, video and PDF, but no real Coffee 26 food photos. Hero and all 53 individual menu illustrations are generated from the supplied menu and are explicitly disclosed in the footer, not presented as actual Coffee 26 photography. All image data and fonts are stored locally. Replace before real launch with cafe photography if available.

## Accessibility
WCAG AA text targets, semantic navigation, keyboard-operable breakfast carousel and menu, touch targets at least 44px, prefers-reduced-motion, no automatic slide advance, no gesture-only interaction, readable currency and portions. Search matches across all categories, not just the visible tab.
